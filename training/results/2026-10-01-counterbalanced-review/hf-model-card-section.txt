
## Counterbalanced review corpus (2026-10-01)

The v4 corpus crosses harmful/redundant removal with deletion/no-op replacement
in six families. It contains 52 review examples and 24 readout-only voice/code
examples. Native notorch joint training completed 100 updates in 5,731.93 seconds;
the unchanged checkpoint selector chose update 100. The source and binary match
the previous joint run. Each update covers 52 decision and 1,012 remaining-answer
targets. No Torch training or inference is used.

The selected experimental GGUF is
`experiments/counterbalanced-review/counterbalanced-review-selected.gguf`, SHA256
`aa201ef112dc925f3dc34fc1af9f9a51e61610efd5fe99da44f9cb344a19324a`.
Its native adapter/export parity checks pass. The 12 saved adapters, selected
GGUF and three corpus artifacts were downloaded and verified after archival.

Both models answered the same 88 prompts naturally; all 176 complete responses
were manually assessed. A grounded concern requires a genuine issue, causal
citation and support for every material claim.

The v4 model recognizes two causally cited training issues, but an unsupported
separate-product claim spoils the attribution review. Another explanation
recognizes lost input validation while citing the wrong changed line. The
diagnostic close-removal answer receives credit for identifying the sole
required fclose; its awkward wording and a stricter alternate reading are
retained. The latter would remove that full-review credit, without changing
any complete-pair result. All 24 fresh transfer responses are empty.

This checkpoint is not promoted as the runtime model. The data intervention
combines wording repairs and quartets, changes loss denominators, and retains
a protection-count cue in training. Fresh opposite-label transfer pairs have
equal counts. Better empty-answer accuracy does not establish contextual review
competence or prove that the current adapter route received sufficient training.

Full responses, exact native token traces, manual judgments, sensitivities,
source hashes and reproduction instructions are preserved under
`experiments/counterbalanced-review/evidence/` and `source/`. The next recorded
diagnostic is a native affine readout of frozen final-MLP input states with
family grouping, count-feature baselines and paired-label permutations. It has
not been run.
